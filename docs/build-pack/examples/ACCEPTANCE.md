# Port Evidence Desk · Demonstration script

**Input and expected outcome.** Example configured terminal tariff in INR uses local calendar dates, a discharge date of May 1 as free day one, May 2 as free day two, then INR 1,000/day including gate-out date. Carrier bills INR 3,000. Gate-out could be May 4 or 5, so expected charge is INR 2,000–3,000 and potential dispute INR 0–1,000. Under a manager-approved INR 200 evidence ceiling and an explicit policy permitting investigation of that range, a quote of INR 150 is eligible. An authorized May 4 record fixes the variance at INR 1,000. A later carrier credit of INR 700 records actual recovery of INR 700; without the credit recovery is zero. If the evidence quote is denominated in another asset, require a pinned conversion within its validity window or block authorization.

**Operator surfaces.** Show invoice lines alongside a terminal-local day ledger, the original timestamp with offset and its provenance. Expose uncertainty range, quote and cap in their actual currencies, separate confirmed and hypothetical events, and list unresolved conflicts. Display quoted dispute, approved package and confirmed credit as separate totals.

**Failure to demonstrate.** Deduplicate partner records by supplier, record ID and revision. Append contradictory events instead of overwriting. A late correction recomputes a new calculation revision and invalidates review of the old package; preserve the historical export. Reconcile a charged partner order with its original ID after restart. Failed commercial confirmation leaves the paid connector capability blocked.

Create a labeled, rights-cleared sample through the import or creation UI. Identify the seeded input separately from all real actions and receipts. Record with two appropriate role sessions where required, browser refresh and worker restart. Use a final export or read-only case view tied to the input revision; show any unsupported capabilities as such. Record actual outcomes next to expected outcomes in the target repo.
